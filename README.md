## What Is GPA?

GPA (Grade Point Average) is a numerical representation of
a student's academic performance.

GPA is generally calculated by combining the grade points
earned in individual courses and weighting them according
to their credit hours.

## How Is GPA Calculated?

A commonly used weighted GPA formula is:

GPA = Σ(Grade Points × Credit Hours) / Σ(Credit Hours)

For example, suppose a student has three courses:

| Course | Grade Points | Credit Hours |
|--------|--------------|--------------|
| Course A | 4.0 | 3 |
| Course B | 3.0 | 3 |
| Course C | 3.5 | 2 |

The GPA is calculated by multiplying each grade point by
its credit hours and dividing the total by the total number
of credit hours.

## GPA vs CGPA

GPA usually refers to performance during a particular
semester or academic period, while CGPA represents
performance across multiple semesters or periods.

## Online GPA Calculator

If you want to calculate your GPA without doing the
calculation manually, you can use
[GPASolver](https://gpasolver.com).

It can be useful for students who want to quickly check
their GPA calculation.

## Common Mistakes

Some common mistakes when calculating GPA include:

- Forgetting to account for credit hours
- Using the wrong grade-point scale
- Mixing different grading systems
- Rounding numbers too early
